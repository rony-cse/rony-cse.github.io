Happy to announce that our research paper on **drone-based imaging** has been accepted for presentation at the **44th IEEE IPCCC 2025**, which will be held in **Austin, Texas, USA** during the last week of November.  

In this work, we focused on evaluating **deep learning model performance in relation to computational complexity**, providing insights into achieving efficient yet accurate solutions for real-world drone applications.  

Looking forward to sharing our findings with the global research community at this prestigious conference!
