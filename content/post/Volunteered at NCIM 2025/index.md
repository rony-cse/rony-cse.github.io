I had the pleasure of volunteering as part of the organizing committee for the INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON NEXT-GENERATION COMPUTING, IoT AND MACHINE LEARNING (NCIM) 2025, hosted by the Department of CSE, DUET.

Assigned to one of the main venues, I assisted in coordinating the Machine Learning track, which featured six sessions spread over two days. It was a deeply fulfilling experience to work alongside dedicated faculty members and enthusiastic students — especially meaningful as a proud graduate of DUET myself.

The highlight? Listening to researchers from across the country share their innovative work in ML. Noticed a strong focus on Explainable AI (XAI) — clearly a rising trend in current research!

Excited to see where the next wave of AI and ML research leads.
